Use the chart action for BIST chart requests.
For current chart requests, call `/chart` with `mode=current`.
For important/decision-sensitive current chart requests, call `mode=safe_current`.
For dated or multi-month requests, call `mode=balanced`.

Only treat `screenshot_url` as a chart image when `chart_status` begins with `ok_tradingview` or `ok` and the URL is present.
If `screenshot_url` is null, say the chart image could not be captured and do not perform visual chart analysis.
Midas/Bloomberg quote data is only secondary price/context verification, never a substitute for a candle chart.
